# WashDesk Google AI Growth Studio — Owner Setup

## 1. Create the free AI key
1. Open https://openrouter.ai/keys.
2. Sign in or create an OpenRouter account.
3. Create an API key. Do not share it in chat, screenshots, WhatsApp or source code.
4. Open WashDesk v24 → Google Reviews → AI Growth Studio.
5. Paste the key into “OpenRouter API key”, select “Save key”, then “Test free AI”.
6. Repeat on each device that should generate AI content. The key is stored on that device only; it is not synced across phones or put in backups.

## 2. Draft a Google review reply
1. Open Google Business Profile manager and choose PRIME AUTO CARE.
2. Open Read reviews and copy the customer's review.
3. Paste it in WashDesk → Manual reply workspace.
4. Choose “Generate reply draft” in AI Growth Studio.
5. Check the wording, edit if necessary, choose “Copy reply”.
6. Return to Google's reply box and publish it there.

AI drafts are not automatically published. Complaint, refund, safety or uncertain cases should always be checked carefully.

## 3. Generate local keywords
1. Choose “Generate local keywords”.
2. Review the phrases and their relevance rationale.
3. Open the Google Trends comparison link for a phrase to compare relative interest in Gujarat/India.
4. Use only terms that truthfully describe the services PRIME AUTO CARE actually offers.

The score is an AI relevance estimate, not measured search volume. This release does not claim exact monthly search counts.

## 4. Generate two Google post drafts
1. Choose “Generate today's 2 posts”.
2. Read both drafts and correct every business fact, service, hour and price.
3. Attach a real JPG or PNG photo of the business/work. The app accepts 10 KB–5 MB and requires at least 250 × 250 pixels.
4. Save edits.
5. For manual posting, choose “Copy & open Google”, paste the text in Google Business Profile and upload the original photo there.
6. Use the direct Publish/Schedule action only after Google OAuth and Business Profile API access have been approved and verified.

The app does not automatically fabricate photos, customer stories, discounts, vehicle work or before/after results. The owner should check every post before publication.

## 5. Know the free limits
OpenRouter currently lists free model variants at 50 requests/day and 20 requests/minute. Limits can change, and free providers may be unavailable or busy. The app allows only free model routes; it does not silently switch to paid models.

The system creates drafts when the owner presses the generation buttons. It is not a 24/7 unattended agent and does not generate content while the app is closed.

## 6. What still needs Google approval
- Google Business Profile API Basic Access approval for the project.
- OAuth consent and credentials configured securely in Supabase.
- A successful connection to the correct PRIME AUTO CARE location.
- A real test of review sync, reply publication or post publication before enabling direct API workflows.

Until that is completed, use manual Google publication. Do not interpret a saved draft or copied reply as a published Google update.

## 7. Website and future booking work
Public website path: https://zaid404sec.github.io/zaid404sec/website/

After the Pages workflow reports deployment success, open the URL on a phone, check the service details and then add it in Google Business Profile → Edit profile → Contact → Website.

Booking and customer advance payments are a separate next phase. Do not collect booking advances through an untested feature; the next phase needs a booking calendar, capacity rules, advance ledger, refund/reschedule rules and invoice allocation.
